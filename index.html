Welcome! I am an undergraduate researcher at the University of California, Santa Cruz, pursuing a B.S. in Physics (Astrophysics) with a minor in History of Consciousness. I conduct research at Johns Hopkins University through the Rowland Summer Research Fellowship (top 5% of applicants; invited back for a second summer), where I am studying the mechanisms of galaxy quenching using spatially resolved SDSS MaNGA IFU data.

My research focuses on how galaxies die: specifically, the cool and warm interstellar gas (Na I D and Ca II) in 140 red geyser galaxies at z ∼ 0.04, and the connections between gas inflows, AGN activity, and galaxy interactions. I have two first-author papers — one published in The Astrophysical Journal (2026) and one in preparation — under the mentorship of Prof. Timothy Heckman (JHU), Dr. Namrata Roy, Prof. Kevin Bundy (UCSC), and Dr. Kyle Westfall. I was 1 of 32 researchers (∼1% of attendees) invited to the 247th AAS Press Conference, and I am a Barry M. Goldwater Scholar.

Get in touch if you are interested in collaborating or discussing my research!
