About

A quick pronunciation guide to my name: it is pronounced as Uh - Ree - Yan.

I am a third year undergraduate student and Goldwater Scholar pursuing a B.S. in Astrophysics at the University of California, Santa Cruz , with a minor in History of Consciousness, which is a mix of philosophy, critical theory, and political theory. My experience in doing astrophysics research spans a diverse range of topics, spanning from theory-based simulations for modeling the atmospheric escape in exoplanets to analyzing observational data for probing the fundamental mechanisms behind galaxy evolution. This has allowed me to not only explore different areas of research and better understand the subfields that I am most interested in, but it has also given me the opportunity to approach solving problems from different perspectives and utilizing tools from across subfields. Through these experiences, I have developed a strong interest in galaxy-supermassive black hole co-evolution and grown my expertise in Python, statistics, and using physics to explain observations.

After my Ph.D., I ultimately plan to become a professor of astrophysics. In addition to my research ambitions, I also aspire to be a great mentor. Transformative discoveries are made by decades of collaborative work, and I believe it is crucial to get the interest of the next generation of scientists, while expanding science to those historically marginalized.

I was born and grew up in Esfahan, Iran. Unfortunately, because of politics, there are a lot of misconceptions about Iran, but I can honestly say that Iranians are some of the nicest people out there :)

In my free time, I like to go on a long run on a beautiful track in Santa Cruz, or watch soccer (Real Madrid) and NBA (Warriors).
