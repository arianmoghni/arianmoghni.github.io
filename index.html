Welcome! I am a third-year undergraduate at the University of California, Santa Cruz studying Astrophysics, with a minor in History of Consciousness (philosophy, critical theory, and political theory).

I have a wide range of research experiences, from theory-based simulations of atmospheric escape in exoplanets to analyzing observational data for RR Lyrae stars or understanding quenching in galaxies. I primarily work with Prof. Timothy Heckman, Dr. Namrata Roy, Prof. Kevin Bundy, and Dr. Kyle Westfall, and use spatially resolved integral field spectroscopy to trace interstellar gas in quiescent systems and better understand the connection between AGN feedback, environments of galaxies, and quenching. I have published a first-author paper on this research in The Astrophysical Journal and drafting another first-author paper to be published within the coming months. I am also a Goldwater Scholar.

Outside of research, I do an extensive amount of outreach. I write daily posts and articles about physics and astronomy in Farsi online to help break language barriers, last year receiving more than 500,000 post views (prior to internet shutdowns in Iran). One of my pieces of writing won 4th place in a writing competition at the Griffith Observatory and has been published in the Griffith Observer magazine. Additionally, I mentor and tutor students from underprivileged backgrounds as a volunteer.

I plan to pursue a Ph.D. in astrophysics and ultimately become a professor of physics and astronomy to advance our understanding of the formation and evolution of galaxies, supermassive black holes, and the link between the two, while also mentoring and teaching the next generation of scientists.

Feel free to reach out if you want to chat about research or collaborate!
